# LIGHT — Minimal Structural Analysis v0.1

## Question
What is the smallest structure that must exist for light to be described consistently?

## Layer 1 — Field
A_mu(x) provides the electromagnetic gauge potential.

## Layer 2 — Relation
Derivatives connect field values at neighboring spacetime points. Local gauge covariance requires a connection through D_mu.

## Layer 3 — Difference / curvature
F_muν captures the antisymmetric local difference of the potential. This is the electromagnetic field strength.

## Layer 4 — Dynamics
The action selects allowed histories/configurations through the equations of motion.

## Layer 5 — Quantization
The allowed field modes become quantum degrees of freedom. Photon states are excitations of the electromagnetic field.

## Layer 6 — Interaction
Charged matter couples to A_mu. The coupling strength is set by electric charge; the dimensionless electromagnetic coupling α is approximately 1/137 at low energy, while its effective value runs with energy.

## Layer 7 — Observation
Experiments access gauge-invariant consequences: energy, momentum, polarization, transition rates, scattering probabilities, detector events, etc.

## Current structural skeleton

state → relation → local difference → constraint/dynamics → excitation → interaction → measurable change

## Critical result
The deepest useful candidate found so far is not "photon = edge". A safer statement is:

**physical electromagnetic behavior is generated by fields subject to symmetry constraints and local relations.**

The photon is a quantized excitation within this structure.
