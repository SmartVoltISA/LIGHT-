# LIGHT — Lagrangian Structure

## Electromagnetic sector

L_EM = −1/4 F_muν F^muν

with

F_muν = ∂_mu A_ν − ∂_ν A_mu.

The action is the spacetime integral of the Lagrangian density. Stationarity of the action gives the field equations.

## QED

A compact form is

L_QED = ψ-bar(i γ^mu D_mu − m)ψ − 1/4 F_muν F^muν.

The first term describes the charged matter field and its kinetic/mass structure. The second describes the electromagnetic field. The covariant derivative contains the interaction.

## Structural decomposition

1. State space: fields ψ and A_mu.
2. Symmetry: local U(1) gauge invariance.
3. Differential relation: D_mu.
4. Curvature/field strength: F_muν.
5. Dynamics: action principle.
6. Coupling: electric charge q.
7. Quantum excitations: photons and charged-particle states.
8. Observables: gauge-invariant measurable quantities.

## Conservation

Continuous symmetries are connected to conservation laws through Noether's theorem. In the electromagnetic theory, gauge symmetry is tied to charge conservation, while spacetime translation symmetries lead to energy-momentum conservation.

## LIGHT question

If the most compact description is built from fields + symmetry + differential relations + action, then the minimal architectural unit may not be an object. It may be a constrained relation between possible states.

This is a research hypothesis, not a replacement for QED.
