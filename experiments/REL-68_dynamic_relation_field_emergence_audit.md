# REL-68 — Dynamic relation → field → interaction audit

## Question

Can a field-like description emerge from relations whose local state can change and propagate influence, without postulating electromagnetic E/B fields at the start?

## Ω starting point

We distinguish four things:

`RELATION` — structural relation between degrees of freedom.

`SIGNAL` — a change that propagates through local relations.

`ENERGY` — a conserved/transferable quantity associated with physical dynamics; it is not identified with information.

`FIELD` — a local state variable that summarizes the influence/dynamical state carried by the distributed relation network.

The experiment deliberately does not begin with Maxwell equations.

## Minimal construction

Take a 1D chain of local cells. Each cell carries two dynamical quantities `(A,B)`. Neighboring cells are coupled only to nearest neighbors.

Define the local first-order evolution:

`∂t A = α ∂x B`

`∂t B = β ∂x A`.

For `αβ > 0`, the system has finite characteristic speeds

`v = ±√(αβ)`.

The state `(A,B)` is therefore not merely a static relation label: its local change propagates through neighboring relations.

## Energy-like invariant

Choose the quadratic density

`u = (β A² + α B²)/2`

for positive `α,β`. Under the corresponding local evolution, the total integral is conserved for suitable boundary conditions. The conserved quantity is therefore associated with the dynamics, not merely with the descriptive label of the state.

## Interaction interpretation

Introduce a localized material variable `q(x,t)` coupled to the field state through a local interaction term. A change in `(A,B)` changes the local equation for `q`; conversely, changing `q` changes the field source.

This establishes the structural pattern:

`RELATION → LOCAL DYNAMICAL STATE → PROPAGATING CHANGE → LOCAL INTERACTION → MATTER RESTRUCTURING`.

The field variables are useful because they compress the state of many local relations into quantities defined at each location.

## Electromagnetic positive control

Vacuum Maxwell propagation has the same first-order two-component principal form after reduction to a transverse 1D sector, with characteristic speed

`c = 1/√(μ0 ε0)`.

Thus electromagnetic propagation is a concrete physical realization of the broader field/interaction pattern.

## Countermodels

The same structural package is also realized by non-electromagnetic systems such as acoustic waves or generic symmetric hyperbolic systems.

Therefore:

`locality + finite propagation + positive conserved quadratic energy + local interaction`

**does not uniquely select electromagnetism.**

## Result

Conditional positive for the emergence of a field-like dynamical description:

`RELATION`
→ `LOCAL STATE OF RELATIONS`
→ `LOCAL DYNAMICS`
→ `PROPAGATING INFLUENCE`
→ `FIELD-LIKE DESCRIPTION`
→ `INTERACTION`
→ `STRUCTURAL / MATERIAL CHANGE`.

But negative for direct derivation of electromagnetism.

The experiment shows that the conceptual move from static relation to dynamical field is mathematically coherent and physically meaningful, while Maxwell/E-M specificity requires additional constraints.

## Critical boundaries

Not derived:

- Maxwell equations uniquely
- electric/magnetic interpretation of A and B
- ε0 and μ0
- SI scale
- universal value of c
- Lorentz invariance
- U(1) gauge group
- quantum photon
- matter charge
- a unique microscopic ontology of field

The conserved quadratic quantity is energy-like within the model. Identifying it with physical electromagnetic energy requires the electromagnetic theory and units.

**CONDITIONAL POSITIVE + ELECTROMAGNETIC BOUNDARY.**
