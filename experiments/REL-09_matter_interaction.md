# REL-09 — Matter Coupling, Absorption and Emission

## Physical target

Electromagnetic radiation exchanges energy and momentum with charged matter. In QED the interaction is represented by coupling the charged matter field to the electromagnetic gauge connection, schematically through `D_mu = partial_mu + i q A_mu`.

Absorption is a transition in which radiation energy is transferred to matter. Emission is the reverse transition. Scattering changes the state/momentum/direction while respecting the relevant conservation laws.

## Relation-first mapping

`field state ↔ matter state → coupling relation → transition → measurable energy/momentum/state change`.

## Status

`KNOWN / ARCHITECTURAL COMPATIBILITY`

The existence of coupling is established physics. The Ω claim that coupling itself is reducible to a more primitive relation schema remains open.

## Hard test

A stronger REL-09 numerical/quantum test must use a fixed matter Hamiltonian and compare predicted transition rates or scattering probabilities against established QED results without post-hoc parameter fitting.
