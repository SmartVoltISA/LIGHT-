# REL-59 — Commutant vs Relational Redundancy Audit

## Question

Can the Ω principle

`independent relational content ↔ physical distinction`

turn the algebraic condition

`commutant = scalars`

into a derived irreducibility criterion?

## Construction

Take a 4-dimensional Clifford generating set `Γ_μ` and duplicate it:

`Γ'_μ = Γ_μ ⊗ I₂`.

Operators acting only on the multiplicity factor commute with every `Γ'_μ`:

`X = I₄ ⊗ σ_i`.

Therefore the doubled representation has a non-scalar commutant.

For the single 4-dimensional block, the same non-scalar candidates do not commute with the full Clifford generating set.

## Result

The doubled construction contains exact transformations that preserve all tested dynamical generators while mixing the two copies.

This is the algebraic signature expected from multiplicity/reducibility.

But the crucial distinction remains unresolved:

`non-scalar commutant → symmetry`

does **not** by itself imply

`symmetry → gauge redundancy`.

The same mathematical symmetry can be treated as an internal physical symmetry unless an additional physical rule identifies it as unobservable/redundant.

Therefore:

`commutant > scalars`

is evidence for additional invariant structure, but it does not by itself classify that structure as either a physical degree of freedom or a gauge redundancy.

## Ω interpretation

This sharpens REL-56–58.

A relationally identical duplicate is a strong candidate for quotienting.

A non-scalar commutant is stronger: it exhibits transformations invisible to the tested dynamical relations. However, invisibility to one relational algebra is not automatically physical redundancy. One must specify the complete observable relation set or an equivalent criterion.

Candidate closure:

`DISTINCTION → RELATION → STATE → HISTORY`

`→ observable relational algebra`

`→ equivalence / quotient`

`→ commutant test`

`→ irreducibility`

The missing piece is the definition of the **complete physical relation algebra**. Without it, the distinction between a gauge redundancy and an internal physical symmetry cannot be derived.

## Status

**Negative for direct Ω → irreducibility. Positive for identifying the precise missing object: the complete observable relational algebra.**

## External mathematical anchor

Schur-type results connect irreducibility with the triviality of the commutant in standard representation-theoretic settings. The audit does not claim to derive that theorem; it tests whether Ω can independently supply the physical interpretation of the commutant.
