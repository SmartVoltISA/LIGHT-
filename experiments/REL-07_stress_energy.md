# REL-07 — Energy, Momentum and Stress

## Physical target

Energy alone is insufficient. Electromagnetic fields carry energy, momentum and stress. In vacuum the Poynting vector gives energy flux, while momentum density is related to the energy flux by `g = S/c^2`.

The Maxwell stress tensor supplies the spatial momentum-flux/stress structure. A complete local conservation statement therefore has the form:

`field energy + field momentum + boundary flux + matter exchange = conserved total`.

## Relation-first mapping

`field state → local field relations → energy density / flux → momentum density → stress → conservation law`.

The same field relations that generate propagation must account for transfer across a boundary and momentum exchange with matter.

## Result

REL-02E already verifies the energy-density/flux layer for a minimal transverse mode. REL-07 extends the required bookkeeping conceptually to momentum and stress. A full 3D numerical stress-tensor test remains a separate computational task.
