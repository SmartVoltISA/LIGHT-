# REL-10 — Quantum Modes and Photon

## Physical target

Classical Maxwell theory describes continuous field amplitudes. Quantization promotes independent normal modes to quantum harmonic oscillators. A photon is a one-quantum excitation of a mode; occupation number can be `n = 0,1,2,...`.

The mode energy is

`E_n = (n + 1/2) hbar omega`.

For a photon excitation above vacuum, `E = hbar omega = h nu` and, in vacuum, `p = hbar k` with `omega = c|k|`.

## Relation-first mapping

`field configuration → mode relations → independent normal modes → quantization → occupation state → measurement statistics`.

## What has been tested

REL-05 freezes the classical massless dispersion layer. REL-10 establishes the quantum mapping from that mode structure to photon occupation as the standard QFT description.
