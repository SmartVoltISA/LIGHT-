# REL-06 — Polarization and Helicity

## Physical target

For a vacuum plane wave with wave-vector `k`, the electromagnetic field is transverse:

`k·E = 0`, `k·B = 0`, and `E·B = 0`.

The physical photon has two independent polarization/helicity states, conventionally `helicity = ±1`.

## Relation-first mapping

Propagation direction `k` is a global relation constraint. The physical field state lives in the transverse subspace. Linear, circular and elliptical polarization are different relations between the two transverse components.

Circular polarization is especially useful because the handed phase rotation maps directly to helicity.

The REL-02E numerical structure already uses `E_y` and `H_z` with propagation along `x`, giving a transverse mode. REL-06 freezes the stronger statement: a 3D Maxwell field has two independent physical transverse photon polarizations after gauge redundancy/constraints are removed.
