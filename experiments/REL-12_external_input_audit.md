# REL-12 — External Input Audit

## Question

Which ingredients of the established light/QED description are actually produced by the relation-first architecture, and which are still supplied externally?

## Frozen distinction

A quantity is **derived** only if it follows from the frozen Ω rules without importing the target physical structure in disguise.

A quantity is **compatible** if the Ω architecture can represent it and reproduce its established equations after the relevant physical ingredients are supplied.

A quantity is **external** if its value, group, representation, dynamics, or quantum rule is inserted from established physics.

## Critical result

The architecture currently survives a large compatibility stack, but the audit finds no justified claim that Ω alone generates the electromagnetic theory.

The strongest unresolved target is therefore a **uniqueness test**:

`frozen Ω axioms -> allowed local transformations -> minimal connection -> invariant -> admissible dynamics`

The test must reject architectures that require arbitrary insertion of U(1), Maxwell dynamics, spacetime geometry, or quantum commutation rules.

## Computational cross-checks

For a local complex state q and arbitrary local phase alpha(x), raw finite differences fail to transform covariantly under spatially varying alpha. Introducing a compensating link U restores covariance to numerical precision (~2.3e-16 in the tested lattice example).

For an additive lattice connection A, the plaquette curvature remains invariant under A_mu -> A_mu + alpha(x)-alpha(x+mu), with maximum numerical difference ~1.1e-15.

These results strengthen the statement that a connection-like object is structurally required for local representation-independent comparison. They do **not** select U(1) uniquely; the complex phase representation was used only as a controlled test of the already-known U(1) case.

## Falsification pressure

The Ω claim is weakened if:

1. a simpler local comparison rule remains representation-independent under nontrivial spatially varying transformations without a connection;
2. the same frozen axioms admit many inequivalent dynamics with no selection principle;
3. U(1) cannot be selected without explicitly assuming a complex phase group;
4. quantum discreteness cannot be obtained without an independent quantization postulate;
5. the architecture has no route to dimensionless coupling strength.

## Status

**REL-12: AUDIT COMPLETE / DERIVATION NOT ACHIEVED.**

The experiment successfully identifies where the current architecture stops being explanatory and starts importing established physics. This boundary is now explicit and testable.
