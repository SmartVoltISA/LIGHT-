# REL-50 — Spin–Statistics Emergence Audit

## Question
Can fermionic statistics be selected from the structural package already accumulated, rather than inserted as an independent postulate?

## Candidate package
- 3+1 dimensional relativistic/Poincare-covariant QFT
- positive-energy spectrum / Hamiltonian bounded below
- positive-definite physical Hilbert space
- vacuum state
- locality (microcausality) at spacelike separation
- fields of definite spin

## Test logic
The spin–statistics theorem states, under the above type of assumptions, that integer-spin fields require Bose commutators while half-integer-spin fields require Fermi anticommutators. This is a theorem of relativistic QFT, not a separate empirical matching rule.

The important Ω boundary is therefore:

STRUCTURAL INPUTS
→ relativistic covariance
→ positive energy
→ positive norm
→ vacuum
→ spacelike locality
→ definite spin
→ CONSISTENT QUANTIZATION
→ statistics sign

For half-integer spin:

spin = 1/2, 3/2, ...
→ commutator choice is inconsistent with the full package
→ anticommutator choice is the consistent branch
→ fermionic statistics.

For integer spin:

spin = 0, 1, 2, ...
→ anticommutator choice is inconsistent with the full package
→ commutator choice is the consistent branch
→ bosonic statistics.

## Countermodels / failure modes
If positivity, locality, relativistic covariance, or the standard Hilbert-space assumptions are removed, wrong-statistics constructions can be written. This confirms that the theorem depends on the full structural package.

Gauge-fixed auxiliary fields are not counterexamples because their indefinite metric is not the physical Hilbert space.

In 2+1 dimensions the ordinary 3+1 spin-statistics conclusion is not the complete story because braid statistics can occur; this audit is explicitly 3+1D.

## Ω interpretation
REL-48 established that U(1) coupling does not select spin 1/2.
REL-49 established that a suitable first-order relativistic carrier can lead to Clifford/Dirac structure once those dynamical requirements are imposed.
REL-50 now shows that once half-integer spin is established, fermionic statistics is not an additional arbitrary choice: it follows from the relativistic QFT consistency package.

Therefore:

U(1) ↛ spin 1/2
spin 1/2 + relativistic locality + positivity + Poincare covariance → Fermi statistics

This is a conditional derivation, not a derivation of spin 1/2 itself.

## External verification
CERN materials explicitly state the integer-spin/boson and half-integer-spin/fermion connection and discuss the spin-statistics theorem. The standard theorem requires relativistic covariance, positive energy/norm, and locality in a suitable QFT framework.

## Boundary
Not derived here:
- 3+1 dimensional spacetime
- Lorentz/Poincare symmetry itself
- positive Hilbert-space metric
- existence of a vacuum
- half-integer spin itself
- the empirical field content of the Standard Model
