# REL-11 — Interference, Coherence and Information

## Physical target

Interference is a consequence of superposition of coherent field amplitudes. Observable intensity contains cross terms, so relative phase between alternatives is physically relevant. Coherence measures the stability/correlation of these phase relations.

## Relation-first mapping

`alternative states → relative phase relation → superposition → observable cross term → distinguishability/information`.

A phase that is globally common to all amplitudes is not observable in ordinary interference; relative phase is.

## Quantum boundary

For single-photon and other nonclassical states, the relevant objects are field operators and correlation functions rather than classical intensities alone. Therefore information cannot be reduced to a classical wave amplitude.

## Status

`KNOWN / ARCHITECTURAL COMPATIBILITY`

The Ω hypothesis is that information is naturally represented by stable distinctions/relations between physically accessible states. This is a structural hypothesis, not a new information-theory law.
