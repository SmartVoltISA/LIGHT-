# REL-41 — 3D transverse-mode selection audit

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Branch:** `rel-41-actual`  
**Status:** structural selection result with explicit boundary

## Question

Can an Ω-style package force the electromagnetic principal structure from:

`DISTINCTION → RELATION → STATE → ORDERED TRANSITION → LOCALITY → BOUNDED INFLUENCE`

plus:

- 3D rotational isotropy;
- first-order local field evolution;
- finite propagation;
- positive-energy-compatible field dynamics;
- exactly two propagating transverse polarizations;
- no inserted Maxwell equations, `E/B` interpretation, `U(1)`, `epsilon_0`, `mu_0`, or SI scale?

## Principal-symbol construction

For two local vector fields `A` and `B`, the isotropic first-order derivative operators available at principal order are generated by the identity and the curl operator. For a plane wave with propagation direction `k-hat = z`,

`C v = k-hat × v`

has eigenvalues `+i, -i, 0`: two transverse directions and one longitudinal direction.

A curl-coupled two-vector family is

`∂t A = alpha curl B`

`∂t B = -beta curl A`.

The transverse characteristic speed is proportional to

`v = sqrt(alpha beta)`.

The longitudinal curl sector is identically zero, so no longitudinal propagating mode is generated by this principal part.

## Countermodels

### Countermodel A — identity coupling

`∂t A = u B`

`∂t B = u A`

This is local and rotationally isotropic but propagates longitudinal as well as transverse components. It therefore fails the exact-transversality requirement.

### Countermodel B — explicit longitudinal propagation

`∂t A = u A`

`∂t B = -v B`

This makes the longitudinal sector dynamical and is therefore excluded by the two-transverse-polarization requirement.

These countermodels show that locality, isotropy and finite characteristic structure alone are insufficient.

## Selection result

Within the restricted local two-vector first-order isotropic ansatz, imposing:

`exactly two propagating polarizations`

and

`no propagating longitudinal sector`

forces the derivative sector toward a **curl-only transverse class**.

That is a substantially stronger result than REL-40. The causal cone is no longer the only special feature; the dimensional structure of the propagating field sector matters.

However, this still does **not** uniquely derive Maxwell electromagnetism.

The surviving family contains

`alpha * beta = constant`

as the cone-setting combination, while `alpha/beta` remains a field-normalization / impedance-like freedom.

Example family:

`(alpha,beta) = (1,1), (4,0.25), (2,0.5), (8,0.125)`

all have

`sqrt(alpha beta) = 1`.

Thus the same causal cone admits different field normalizations.

## Interpretation

The current structural chain is therefore:

`locality`
→ `bounded influence`
→ `finite causal cone`
→ `3D isotropy`
→ `vector field sector`
→ `exactly two propagating polarizations`
→ `no longitudinal propagation`
→ `curl-only principal class`
→ `Maxwell-like structure`

The last arrow must be written **Maxwell-like**, not `Maxwell`, because the coefficient normalization and electromagnetic interpretation remain free.

## Boundary conditions

Not derived here:

- Maxwell equations in their complete sourced form;
- `E` and `B` as physical interpretations of the two fields;
- local `U(1)` gauge redundancy;
- gauge potential `A_mu` and curvature `F_mu nu`;
- `epsilon_0` and `mu_0`;
- SI units;
- numerical `c`;
- Lorentz invariance as a fundamental axiom;
- quantum photon interpretation.

These remain separate research targets.
