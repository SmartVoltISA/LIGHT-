# REL-52 — First-order causal-carrier countermodels

## Question

Does finite causal propagation plus local first-order evolution uniquely select a Dirac/Clifford carrier?

## Test

Construct local first-order hyperbolic systems with finite characteristic speed and positive quadratic norm, but without imposing a four-dimensional Clifford factorization.

A two-component system with principal Hamiltonian

`H = [[0,1],[1,0]]`

has eigenvalues `+1,-1`. A direct sum of two such sectors has the same finite characteristic speeds and a positive quadratic norm.

These are valid first-order local carriers, but they do not constitute a 3+1D Lorentz spinor representation and no four-index gamma system is selected by the causal cone alone.

## Result

The countermodels establish:

`finite causal cone + local first-order evolution + positive quadratic norm`
`↛ unique Dirac/Clifford structure`.

This is consistent with the broader fact that first-order relativistic systems can have different internal representations and that Maxwell and Dirac systems share hyperbolic causal structure without being the same representation.

## Relation to REL-51

REL-51 established the positive conditional statement:

`first-order linear symbol + exact scalar second-order factorization`
`→ Clifford algebra`.

REL-52 establishes the complementary negative statement:

`first-order causal propagation`
`↛ exact scalar factorization`
`↛ Clifford uniquely.

Therefore the missing ingredient is not merely “first order” or “finite causal speed”. The additional requirement is specifically an exact scalar factorization of the relativistic quadratic operator, or an equivalent structural principle.

Neither the causal cone nor generic first-order hyperbolicity supplies the `?` automatically.

**NEGATIVE for unique emergence from the weaker package.**
