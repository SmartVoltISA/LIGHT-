# LIGHT — Quantum Field Theory Core

## Status
Physical foundation. Interpretation is kept separate from established theory.

## 1. Field first
In quantum field theory, light is described by the electromagnetic field. A photon is a quantum excitation of that field, not a classical particle moving through an underlying material medium.

## 2. Maxwell field
The electromagnetic potential is A_mu. The gauge-invariant field strength is

F_muν = ∂_mu A_ν − ∂_ν A_mu.

The free electromagnetic Lagrangian is

L_EM = −1/4 F_muν F^muν.

Its Euler–Lagrange equations give Maxwell's equations in covariant form.

## 3. Matter coupling
For a charged Dirac field ψ, local U(1) invariance replaces the ordinary derivative with

D_mu = ∂_mu + i q A_mu.

The interaction term is proportional to q ψ-bar γ^mu A_mu ψ. The electromagnetic field therefore enters the dynamics through a local coupling to charged matter.

## 4. Quantization
Quantizing the electromagnetic field produces photon creation and annihilation operators. Physical photon states have two transverse polarization/helicity degrees of freedom. The photon is massless in QED.

## 5. What is fundamental here?
The mathematical construction does not begin with a little object called a photon. It begins with a field, its symmetry, its allowed dynamics, and its coupling to matter. The photon appears after quantization as the elementary excitation of that field.

## 6. LIGHT architectural observation
Candidate chain:

field → symmetry constraint → local connection → field strength → excitation → interaction → measurable change

This is an architectural mapping, not a claim that physics literally uses the terms node/edge/connection.

## 7. Verification boundary
Established: Maxwell theory, U(1) gauge structure, QED, photon quantization.

Not established: identifying gauge connection with the universal "relation" of Ω. That remains a hypothesis requiring independent tests.
