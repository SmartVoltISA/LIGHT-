# LIGHT — Derivation Chain

This document records the conceptual derivation without claiming that each step is logically inevitable in all formulations.

1. Relativistic quantum matter is represented by fields.
2. Charged matter has a local phase symmetry U(1).
3. Making that symmetry local requires a covariant derivative.
4. The covariant derivative introduces A_mu.
5. Gauge-invariant dynamics require F_muν = ∂_mu A_ν − ∂_ν A_mu.
6. The Maxwell/QED Lagrangian determines field dynamics and coupling.
7. Quantization of the electromagnetic field yields photon excitations.
8. Photons and charged matter participate in electromagnetic processes.
9. Experiments measure invariant consequences rather than gauge-dependent potentials directly.

## Structural compression

U(1) local symmetry
→ connection A_mu
→ curvature F_muν
→ action
→ quantum modes
→ photon
→ interaction
→ observation

## Why this matters

The photon is not the first item in the derivation. The field-theoretic structure comes first. This makes the photon a state/excitation of an already-defined relational field structure.

## Caution

This compression is a conceptual map. It does not replace the formal derivation of QED, and it does not imply that gauge symmetry is an emergent property of Ω.
